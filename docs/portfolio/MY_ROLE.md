# My role

I conceived, designed, built, ran, analyzed, documented, and presented ContextLab. It is my complete
research and engineering project. I own the question, method, implementation, evidence, product
experience, and public narrative.

## I conceived the research

I defined the central question: when should an enterprise AI system retrieve more, remember more,
search more, and when should it stay simple?

I chose the controlled comparison, the NovaLearn synthetic setting, the frozen variables, the
sealed-evaluation boundary, the promotion criteria, and the limits on public claims. I decided that
a failed gate must remain a result and cannot be cleaned away after the fact.

## I designed the system

I designed the separation between corpus and events, strategy adapters, candidate evidence, context
packs, the provider gateway, review gates, and the evidence viewer. I required every displayed metric
to keep its artifact path, JSON pointer, source-run identity, and SHA-256.

I also set the failure behavior. Protected data stays outside the system under test. Unknown sealed
fields fail closed. Provider failures remain failures. A changed artifact loses its old approval.

## I built the product

I implemented the Python research engine, experiment controls, schemas, evidence exports, and the
React and Vite presentation. I decided what the public experience must explain in five minutes and
what the detailed laboratory must preserve for technical inspection.

I made the no-ship decisions. G2 did not promote an advanced retriever. G3 did not promote a memory
policy. F3 and F5 remain accepted-negative demonstrations. Added machinery did not become product
architecture because it looked interesting.

## I ran and analyzed the study

I executed the controlled comparisons, preserved failures and negative results, inspected the saved
artifacts, and interpreted the findings against the preregistered gates. I did not promote a method
because it looked more advanced.

I used AI systems as tools inside this workflow for implementation assistance and independent
checks. I specified their work, integrated and verified every result, and remained responsible for
the complete project. They did not own a component, make a research decision, or replace my
authorship.

## I authored the evidence and presentation

I created the evidence trail, claim boundaries, technical explanation, case study, and interactive
viewer. I audited the evidence and made every final decision. I am the sole human reviewer, which is
a stated study limit rather than the definition of my role.

My approvals bind exact technical or pending-gate hashes. A later byte change does not inherit an
earlier approval. This is why the historical G4 approval does not cover later viewer work, and why
the portfolio release needs its own final packet.

## End-to-end responsibility

I am responsible for:

- the research question and claim scope;
- the benchmark, controls, stop rules, and evaluation method;
- the architecture, implementation, tests, and product direction;
- the experiment runs, analysis, evidence audit, and gate decisions;
- the documentation, interface, public narrative, and release decision.

Model providers and AI systems are development tools and external dependencies. Their use does not
transfer authorship, implementation ownership, research judgment, or project responsibility away
from me.
