# NovaLearn AI: Security and Data Governance Policy

## [NL-007-S01] Policy Scope

This Security and Data Governance Policy defines the mandatory security controls, data handling procedures, and compliance obligations for the NovaLearn AI platform. This policy applies to all environments managed by NovaLearn AI, including development, staging, sandbox, and production tenants. The scope of this policy encompasses all data ingested, processed, or generated by the NovaLearn AI platform, including customer-uploaded documents, generated training content, chat transcripts, audit logs, metadata, and user credentials.

### Regulatory Compliance and Certification Frameworks
To guarantee compliance with modern corporate standards, NovaLearn AI's system controls are designed to align with major international security certifications and trust frameworks:
1. **SOC 2 Type II Compliance**: Our operations undergo annual audits by independent third parties, validating our performance against the Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, and Confidentiality.
2. **HIPAA Security Rule Alignment**: For healthcare accounts, the platform implements specific physical, administrative, and technical safeguards to secure electronic protected health information (ePHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
3. **GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA Adherence**: Data retention, deletion, and consent mapping systems are structured to enforce compliance with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act.

## [NL-007-S02] Data Classification

To ensure appropriate protection levels are applied to different data types, NovaLearn AI classifies all information into four distinct categories. Each category represents a different level of business risk and regulatory compliance concern.

1. **Class 1: Public Data**: Information that has been intentionally published for public consumption, such as marketing materials, public website content, and standard product specifications. Class 1 data requires basic integrity controls but has no confidentiality requirements.
2. **Class 2: Internal-General Data**: Information that is not intended for the public but has a low risk of harm if disclosed. This includes general company announcements, standard templates, and non-sensitive training guides. This data is accessible to all authorized employees.
3. **Class 3: Confidential Data**: Highly sensitive information that could cause operational, financial, or reputational harm if accessed by unauthorized individuals. This includes internal product roadmaps, pricing calculations, vendor contracts, sales pipeline data, and standard customer onboarding playbooks.
4. **Class 4: Restricted Data**: The most sensitive classification, where unauthorized access could result in severe legal liability, regulatory fines, or catastrophic business damage. This includes customer-uploaded intellectual property, personally identifiable information (PII), protected health information (PHI), payment card industry (PCI) data, system encryption keys, security audit logs, and user passwords.

All data ingested into KnowledgeHub is defaulted to Class 4: Restricted Data and is subject to our most stringent security controls unless an administrator explicitly tags the source otherwise. The table below maps data classification categories to their corresponding handling rules, permitted plans, and security requirements:

| Classification | Examples of Data | Permitted Plans | Mandated Storage Control | Redaction Mandate | Encryption Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 1: Public** | Marketing sheets, GA feature lists | All plans | Standard Cloud Storage | None | TLS in transit, Standard S3 rest |
| **Class 2: Internal** | Core announcements, non-sensitive guides | All plans | Partitioned Cloud Storage | Voluntary | TLS in transit, Standard S3 rest |
| **Class 3: Confidential**| Roadmaps, pricing policies, CSM playbooks | Growth, Enterprise | Logically isolated databases | Recommended | TLS 1.3, AES-256 with KMS key |
| **Class 4: Restricted**  | Patient records (PHI), SSO logs, vector SIDs | Enterprise only | Dedicated vector namespaces | Mandatory PII scrub | TLS 1.3, AES-256-GCM + BYOK key |

## [NL-007-S03] Customer Workspace Isolation

NovaLearn AI is built on a multi-tenant cloud architecture. To guarantee the absolute security and privacy of customer data, the platform enforces strict workspace isolation at multiple layers of the system stack.

* **Logical Database Isolation**: Each customer workspace (tenant) is allocated a logically isolated database partition. The system database utilizes row-level security (RLS) policies and tenant ID validation for every query execution. This prevents data leakage by ensuring that a database connection initiated by Tenant A can never retrieve database records belonging to Tenant B.
* **Vector Index Isolation**: Within our vector database cluster, each customer workspace is assigned a distinct collection or vector namespace. When text chunks are converted into embeddings, they are indexed within that tenant's dedicated partition. Semantic search operations are restricted to the query's origin namespace, guaranteeing that TutorChat search results cannot cross tenant boundaries.
* **Encryption at Rest**: All customer data, including uploaded files, database records, and vector indices, is encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Cryptographic keys are managed via a cloud Key Management Service (KMS), with unique keys generated for each customer tenant. Standard KMS keys are rotated automatically on a 365-day cycle. Enterprise customers utilizing Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) configurations can define key rotation intervals down to 90 days.
* **Encryption in Transit**: All connections to the NovaLearn AI platform must be encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 or TLS 1.2). The system blocks all unencrypted HTTP requests, redirecting them to HTTPS.

### Network Isolation and Security Architecture
In addition to database-level partitioning, the hosting network implements strict container-level isolation:
* **VPC Segment Routing**: Customer workspaces operate within isolated subnets inside our cloud provider. Direct network communication between subnets of different tenants is blocked by default at the routing layer.
* **API Access Firewalls**: Incoming HTTP requests are inspected by a Web Application Firewall (WAF) to detect and block SQL injection, cross-site scripting, and credential stuffing attacks.
* **Container Isolation**: Ingestion workers and LLM processing containers run in logically isolated groups. An ingestion worker spinning up to run OCR on Tenant A's documents cannot access memory blocks or file buffers allocated to Tenant B.

## [NL-007-S04] Access Control and RBAC

Enforcing the principle of least privilege is essential for preventing unauthorized internal data exposure. NovaLearn AI requires role-based access control (RBAC) configurations for all production deployments.

* **System Roles**: The platform defines four standard user roles:
  1. *Global Admin*: Full administrative access to system configurations, user provisioning, billing details, audit logs, and all workspaces.
  2. *Workspace Manager*: Full access to manage files, metadata, and folders within a designated workspace, including assigning permissions.
  3. *Course Author*: Permission to access CourseBuilder to generate and edit training modules, quizzes, and lessons using approved documents.
  4. *Learner*: Restricted read-only access. Learners can query TutorChat and complete courses but cannot view administrative panels, upload files, or edit content.
* **Directory Synchronization**: For Growth and Enterprise plans, the platform supports Okta and Azure Active Directory integration. This allows customer administrators to map Active Directory security groups directly to KnowledgeHub document folders.
* **Folder-Level Access Lists**: Administrators must assign explicit folder permissions in KnowledgeHub. If a user belongs to a security group that is not assigned to a folder, the retrieval engine will automatically exclude all documents in that folder from the user's vector search scope.
* **No Bypass Rule**: Under no circumstances may an implementation specialist or customer admin configure the production environment to bypass permission checks. All user queries must be filtered by the active user's permission mask at the API level.

### API Key and Token Governance
Programmatic API integrations are governed by strict credential policies:
* **Short-Lived User Tokens**: User authentication generates JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) that expire after 1 hour. Re-authentication is required to issue fresh tokens.
* **Static API Keys**: API keys for external ingestion scripts must be generated via the Admin Console. They must be stored in secure vaults (such as HashiCorp Vault or AWS Secrets Manager) and rotated every 90 days.
* **IP Whitelisting**: Connections using static API keys must originate from IP address ranges explicitly whitelisted in the Admin Console, preventing key reuse from unauthorized networks.

## [NL-007-S05] Audit Logging

Audit logs are critical for compliance, security incident investigations, and system performance monitoring. NovaLearn AI tracks all actions taken within the system and generates immutable audit trails.

* **Logged Events**: The system logs all administrative logins, user creations, role changes, SSO configurations, API key generations, document uploads, document deletions, permission changes, and direct database queries.
* **Pricing Plan Tiers**:
  * *Starter Plan*: No audit logging features are available. Access is restricted to basic user management logs.
  * *Growth Plan*: Includes standard audit logging. System logs are accessible via the Admin Console for a rolling 90-day period. Customers can view and export CSV logs of user activity and document modifications.
  * *Enterprise Plan*: Includes advanced audit logging with extended retention. Audit logs are retained for a minimum of 365 days. Enterprise customers can configure automated log forwarding to external Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) tools (such as Splunk or Datadog) via webhooks or secure syslog protocols.
* **Integrity Controls**: Audit logs are stored in a read-only, write-once storage container with logical deletion disabled, preventing users or administrators from tampering with or erasing the logs.

## [NL-007-S06] Data Retention and Deletion

NovaLearn AI establishes clear retention and deletion schedules to ensure compliance with global data privacy regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA).

* **Active Subscription Retention**: During the term of an active subscription, customer data is retained indefinitely in the customer's isolated workspace. Administrators can manually delete individual documents in KnowledgeHub, which initiates a soft deletion phase (retained in a recycle bin for 30 days before permanent deletion).
* **Out-of-Scope Sales Claims**: Sales playbooks (`NL-009`) or discovery transcripts may informally suggest that customer data is deleted "immediately on request." However, this policy is the sole authority for operational deletion timelines. Standard deletion is executed within 90 days after contract termination to ensure complete cleanup across all backup archives.
* **Contract Termination Deletion**: Upon the termination or expiration of a customer subscription agreement:
  1. The workspace is transitioned to a suspended state for 30 days, during which the customer can request data extraction.
  2. Following the suspension window, the active databases, vector indexes, and file stores are flagged for deletion.
  3. The permanent deletion of all tenant data from active disks and primary databases is completed within 60 days of suspension (90 days total from contract termination).
  4. Data contained in cold storage backups is deleted according to our standard 30-day backup rotation cycle, ensuring full cryptographic erasure within 120 days of contract termination.
* **Custom Enterprise Deletion**: Enterprise customers can negotiate custom deletion timelines (e.g., immediate 7-day deletion, custom backup retention). Any such custom terms must be explicitly documented in the signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and configured by the engineering operations team.

### Step-by-Step Data Deletion and Cryptographic Erasure Workflow
To ensure that deleted data is completely unrecoverable, the system executes the following multi-stage deletion process:
1. *Tenant Suspension (Day 1 - 30)*: Upon contract termination, the customer tenant is suspended. The application layer blocks all user logins and API calls. Data remains intact but offline, allowing restoration in case of contract renewal.
2. *Logical Deletion Flagging (Day 31)*: The system runs a cron job that marks all database rows and document storage objects associated with the tenant ID for deletion.
3. *Primary Storage Erasure (Day 32 - 60)*: Background workers execute deletion queries. The database rows are purged from physical disk blocks, and raw document blobs are deleted from S3 buckets. The vector namespace collection is deleted from the active cluster, freeing memory resources.
4. *Backup Invalidation and Cryptographic Erasure (Day 61 - 90)*: Cold storage backup blocks are rotated out of active backup storage. To guarantee complete deletion before physical tapes are overwritten, the customer's unique KMS encryption key is deleted from the Key Management Service. This renders any residual ciphertext in cold backups completely unreadable.
5. *Compliance Sign-off (Day 91)*: The system generates a cryptographic Deletion Certificate, signed by the CTO (Leon Grayson) and Security Lead (Priya Raman), confirming complete destruction.

## [NL-007-S07] Sensitive Data Handling

The ingestion of sensitive data (such as PII, PHI, or financial records) into NovaLearn AI represents a significant security and compliance concern. While the platform is capable of processing sensitive text, customers must follow strict guidelines to prevent data leakage.

* **Customer Governance Obligation**: Ingesting sensitive data is allowed only if the customer's governance structure, role-based access controls, and SSO permissions are configured correctly. The customer is solely responsible for ensuring that only authorized users have access to folders containing sensitive files.
* **PII/PHI Upload Restrictions**:
  * *Starter Plan*: Customers are strictly prohibited from uploading documents containing personally identifiable information (PII), protected health information (PHI), or cardholder data. Starter plans lack the audit logs and SSO controls necessary to manage compliance risk.
  * *Growth and Enterprise Plans*: Uploading PII or PHI is permitted, provided that the customer executes a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) (for healthcare accounts) or a customized DPA.
* **Redaction Recommendation**: We strongly recommend that customers run automated redaction tools to strip out social security numbers, credit card numbers, and patient names from manuals or logs before uploading them into KnowledgeHub.

### PHI and PII Redaction Guidelines
To maintain data privacy, customer content authors should ensure the following 18 HIPAA identifiers are redacted from corporate documents before bulk ingestion:
1. Names of individuals, including employee and customer names.
2. Geographic subdivisions smaller than a state (street address, city, zip code).
3. All elements of dates (except year) directly associated with individuals.
4. Telephone and fax numbers.
5. Email addresses.
6. Social Security Numbers (SSNs).
7. Medical record numbers (MRNs).
8. Health plan beneficiary numbers.
9. Account numbers.
10. Certificate/license numbers.
11. Vehicle identifiers and serial numbers.
12. Device identifiers and serial numbers.
13. Web Universal Resource URLs.
14. Internet Protocol (IP) addresses.
15. Biometric identifiers (fingerprints, voice prints).
16. Full-face photographic images.
17. Any unique identifying number, characteristic, or code.
18. Plaintext password strings and cryptographic hashes.

* **PFI (Protected Financial Information)**: Bank routing details, internal passwords, and API keys must never be uploaded to KnowledgeHub under any circumstances. If the system detects plaintext passwords or encryption keys during ingestion, it will automatically quarantine the file and notify the administrator.

## [NL-007-S08] Third-Party Integrations

Integrations with external tools (such as Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint, Slack, and Microsoft Teams) introduce new pathways for data transmission and must be carefully governed.

* **Security Reviews**: All third-party integrations must undergo a security review before being enabled. The review must verify that the integration uses secure OAuth 2.0 authorization codes, respects API rate limits, and does not bypass workspace isolation controls.
* **The Permission Sync Rule**: When configuring document sync connectors (such as SharePoint or Google Drive), the connector must retrieve both the files and their associated access control lists (ACLs). If the connector cannot import permission structures, the files must be imported into a restricted quarantine folder in KnowledgeHub until permissions are mapped manually by a Workspace Manager.
* **Slack Connector Security**: The Slack connector (limited pilot) must be restricted to designated corporate channels. Administrators must ensure that the Slack channels linked to NovaLearn do not contain external guest users who lack corporate directory credentials, preventing unauthorized query access.
* **Teams Integration Design**: Because Microsoft Teams has a complex multi-tenant model and handles granular guest permissions, our integration is currently in the design phase for Q4 2026. This timeline allows our engineering team to design and test permission-matching synchronization engines to guarantee compliance.

## [NL-007-S09] Incident Response Obligations

In the event of a security incident, service outage, or data breach, NovaLearn AI is bound by specific disclosure and mitigation obligations.

* **Incident Severity Classifications**:
  * *Severity 1 (Critical)*: Data breach involving unauthorized access to restricted customer data, or a complete system outage affecting multiple tenants.
  * *Severity 2 (High)*: Partial system outage affecting document ingestion or search indexing, or a localized permission failure within a single workspace.
  * *Severity 3 (Low)*: Minor functional bugs, cosmetic issues, or single-user login failures.
* **Notification Timelines**:
  * In the event of a verified Severity 1 Data Breach, NovaLearn AI will notify affected customer administrators within 72 hours of verification, providing details on the affected files, timeline, and mitigation steps.
  * System outage incidents (such as the March 2026 upload outage) must be documented in a formal Incident Postmortem (`NL-013`) and provided to customers within 5 business days of incident resolution.
* **Corrective Action and Follow-Up**: Following any Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident, our security and engineering teams must complete a root cause analysis, implement corrective controls, and run automated load testing to verify system resilience.

### Incident Management Checklist
Our security team executes a five-stage response procedure during security incidents:
1. **Triage and Classification**: Analyze log data, determine the scope of exposure, and classify severity within 2 hours of detection.
2. **Containment**: Rotate compromised API keys, isolate affected server containers, and suspend compromised database partitions to prevent further leak.
3. **Investigation**: Perform forensics on PostgreSQL and vector database logs, identifying what data blocks were accessed and by which IP address.
4. **Notification**: Send encrypted email notices to affected customer compliance leads, detailing the incident timeline and file list.
5. **Postmortem and Remediation**: Publish a formal incident report outlining the corrective engineering actions to prevent recurrence.

## [NL-007-S10] Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this Security and Data Governance Policy are strictly controlled and must follow a formal approval process.

* **Request Process**: Any request for a policy exception (e.g., temporary exemption from MFA requirements, manual deletion bypass, or custom log retention) must be submitted in writing to the Security and Governance Lead.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: The Security Lead will evaluate the request based on risk level, business necessity, and mitigating controls. Exceptions will not be approved if they compromise customer workspace isolation, violate data privacy regulations, or expose Class 4 Restricted Data to unauthorized users.
* **Approval Authority**:
  * Exceptions with a *Low Risk* rating can be approved by the Security and Governance Lead (Priya Raman).
  * Exceptions with a *Medium or High Risk* rating must be signed off by the Security and Governance Lead and the Chief Technology Officer (CTO: Leon Grayson).
* **Retention of Exception Records**: All approved exceptions must be documented in an active registry, reviewed every 90 days, and revoked immediately upon the expiration of the approved exception period.

### Policy Violations and Compliance Enforcement
Failure to adhere to the security baselines documented herein, or implementing unauthorized policy exceptions, constitutes a serious security violation. The compliance lead conducts quarterly random audits of all tenant configurations. Verified instances of unauthorized group access configurations, bypassing MFA, or uploading restricted Class 4 data onto Starter plans will result in immediate suspension of the affected workspaces and mandatory retraining of customer admins. Severe or persistent violations are reported directly to the NovaLearn Board of Directors and may lead to unilateral service termination.

### Exception Audit Logging and Registry Maintenance

The policy exception registry is hosted in a secure, write-only database schema that is isolated from the standard tenant metadata indexes. The Security Lead (Priya Raman) is the sole custodian of the registry. Every entry in the exception registry must contain the unique Exception ID, requester details, approving authority signature, business justification, risk classification rating, specific compensating controls implemented, and the scheduled expiration date. The Admin Console generates an automated alert 14 days prior to any exception expiration, prompting the requester to submit a renewal request or prepare for immediate revocation. The audit logging engine records every access, edit, or revocation of the exception registry, sending these logs to our centralized SIEM system. These audit trails are reviewed by external SOC 2 Type II auditors during our annual security evaluations to verify that no unauthorized exemptions are active. Any exception that remains active without a valid, signed renewal is flagged as a compliance breach, triggering the standard violation procedures.
